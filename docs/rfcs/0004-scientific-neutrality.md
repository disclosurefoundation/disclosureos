# RFC 0004: Measurements, observables, and interpretations

Status: proposed v2 contract. Owners: observables and origins.

A measurement describes a recorded quantity, unit, reference frame, uncertainty, acquisition conditions, and provenance. An observable assessment explains whether an operational criterion is supported. An origin interpretation proposes an explanation. These are separate objects; none requires the next to exist.

The standard MUST permit ordinary aircraft, background periods, noise, artifacts, and unresolved observations without implying anomalousness. ADS-B association is context; it is not support for low observability. Absence of a detection is interpretable only with sensitivity, coverage, and acquisition context.

Vocabulary entries retain stable IDs and deprecated aliases. An entry's label MUST distinguish recorded behavior from an inferred physical mechanism. Entries include attribution, sources, operational criteria, limitations, and review status. Hypotheses may overlap and need not be exhaustive. Insufficient information is a state of knowledge, not an origin hypothesis.

Testability claims identify an evaluator, date, context, proposed discriminating observation, and limitations. Unsupported blanket true/false flags do not become objective scientific facts. Numerical thresholds remain experimental until qualified review records their scope and rationale. Existing IDs are never silently reassigned when language changes.

The current vocabulary remains available for legacy reproduction. A later vocabulary review must verify primary sources and solicit qualified scientific review before stable publication; this RFC does not assert that such review has occurred.
